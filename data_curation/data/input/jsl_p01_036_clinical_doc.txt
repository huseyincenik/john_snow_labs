SUBJECTIVE:
Patient is a 68-year-old male s/p left PCN tube placement for a benign ureteral stricture. He is now 6 months out from the procedure and has been tolerating the tube. He is eager to attempt internalization. He continues treatment for his prostate cancer with good response.

ASSESSMENT:
- Left ureteral obstruction, successfully managed with a PCN tube.
- Ready to proceed with antegrade intervention.

PLAN:
1.  We have scheduled the patient for an antegrade nephrogram, balloon ureteroplasty (dilation) of the left ureteral stricture, and antegrade placement of a double-J ureteral stent.
2.  The procedure is planned for next week.
3.  If successful, we will cap the nephrostomy tube for a trial period. If he tolerates this without pain or signs of obstruction, the PCN tube can then be removed, leaving him with only the internal stent.
4.  Discussed risks including failure of the procedure, infection, and bleeding. Patient understands and wishes to proceed.
