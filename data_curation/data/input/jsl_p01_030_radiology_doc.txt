EXAMINATION: NM RENAL SCAN W/ DIURETIC (MAG3)
CLINICAL STATEMENT: 67-year-old male with left hydronephrosis. Evaluate for severity of obstruction and differential function.

TECHNIQUE: Following intravenous injection of 8.19 mCi of Tc-99m MAG3, dynamic posterior images of the kidneys were acquired for 40 minutes. At 20 minutes, 40 mg of furosemide was administered intravenously.

FINDINGS:
PERFUSION: Both kidneys are well-perfused.

FUNCTION:
DIFFERENTIAL FUNCTION: Left Kidney: 44%, Right Kidney: 56%.

LEFT KIDNEY: There is mildly delayed tracer uptake and excretion. The collecting system is dilated. Following diuretic administration, there is delayed and incomplete washout, with a half-time (T1/2) clearance of greater than 20 minutes. The renogram curve is consistent with a significant partial obstruction.

RIGHT KIDNEY: Normal uptake and prompt excretion. The T1/2 clearance after diuretic is less than 10 minutes, which is normal.

IMPRESSION:
1.  Split renal function is 44% on the left and 56% on the right. The left kidney still contributes significantly to overall renal function.
2.  The left kidney demonstrates a pattern consistent with a high-grade partial obstruction.

This confirms the functional significance of the left ureteral obstruction and the need for intervention to preserve the remaining function of the left kidney.
