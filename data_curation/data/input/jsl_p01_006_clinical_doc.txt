PATIENT: Patient
ID: p01

SUBJECTIVE:
Patient is a 61-year-old male seen for his first post-treatment follow-up. He completed a course of EBRT (75.6 Gy in 42 fractions) for prostate cancer on April 15, 2016. This was combined with 6 months of ADT (last Lupron injection January 28, 2016).
His oncologic history is notable for Lynch syndrome, rectal cancer s/p APR, and appendiceal cancer.
He reports doing reasonably well. He has ongoing fatigue which he attributes to the ADT, but notes it is slowly improving.
Urinary: IPSS is 9/2 (previously 5/3 pre-treatment), mainly frequency which he associates with caffeine. No hematuria or incontinence.
Sexual: IIEF is 5 (previously 6).
Bowel: Ostomy output is normal.

OBJECTIVE:
Vitals: Stable.
Labs:
- PSA (07/28/2016): <0.05 ng/mL (Nadir)
- Testosterone (07/28/2016): 58 ng/dL (still castrate)

ASSESSMENT:
1.  **Prostate Cancer (C61.9):** Status post definitive EBRT and ADT. Excellent response with PSA nadir <0.05 ng/mL. Currently NED.
2.  **Lynch Syndrome (HNPCC):** Stable. Requires ongoing multi-system surveillance.
3.  **Treatment-related side effects:** Mild urinary frequency (cystitis) and fatigue/sexual dysfunction secondary to ADT.

PLAN:
1.  Continue to monitor PSA and testosterone levels. Next check in 6 months.
2.  Recommend continued surveillance for other Lynch-associated cancers, including annual colonoscopy (of stoma/remaining colon) and continued urologic surveillance.
3.  Manage side effects symptomatically. Advised to reduce caffeine intake.
4.  Return to clinic in 9-12 months, or sooner if any issues arise.
