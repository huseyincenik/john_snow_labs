This was a telemedicine visit conducted via video.

SUBJECTIVE:
Patient is a 65-year-old male seen for routine follow-up of his prostate cancer, s/p EBRT in 2016. He feels well. He is also undergoing surveillance for his high-grade bladder cancer, with his last cystoscopy in November 2019 being negative.

OBJECTIVE:
Labs:
- PSA (02/15/2020): 0.46 ng/mL (Previous: 0.23 in Feb 2019)

ASSESSMENT:
1.  **Prostate Cancer (C61.9):** Continued slow biochemical recurrence. PSA remains low but is clearly rising. Doubling time is approximately 12 months. No indication for salvage therapy at this time.
2.  **Bladder Cancer (C67.9):** Status post TURBT and BCG. Currently with no evidence of recurrence. He is appropriately following up with Urology.
3.  **Lynch Syndrome:** Stable. He is aware of his diagnosis and the need for continued surveillance.

PLAN:
1.  **Prostate Cancer:** Continue active surveillance. The rising PSA is noted, and we will monitor it more closely. Repeat PSA in 6 months. If it continues to rise, we will consider imaging (e.g., PSMA PET) to investigate for local vs. distant recurrence.
2.  **Bladder Cancer:** Continue close follow-up with Dr. Johnson as planned.
3.  Continue all other cancer surveillance as recommended for Lynch Syndrome.
