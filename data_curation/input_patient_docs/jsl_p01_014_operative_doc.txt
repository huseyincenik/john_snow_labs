Title: Operative Report - Re-staging TURBT

PRE-OPERATIVE DIAGNOSIS: High-grade pT1 urothelial carcinoma of the bladder.

POST-OPERATIVE DIAGNOSIS: Same.

PROCEDURE: Re-staging Transurethral Resection of Bladder Tumor (TURBT).

SURGEON: Dr. Johnson, MD

INDICATION: Per standard of care, a re-staging TURBT is performed within 6 weeks of an initial pT1 diagnosis to ensure accurate staging and clear any residual disease before starting intravesical therapy.

FINDINGS AND PROCEDURE:
Cystoscopy revealed post-resection changes at the prior tumor site on the posterior wall. There was some erythematous and bullous-appearing tissue at the margins of the resection bed. The area was re-resected, taking care to obtain tissue from the margins and the deep base of the prior resection site. Hemostasis was achieved. A Foley catheter was placed.

SPECIMENS SENT:
1. Bladder, posterior wall re-resection

PLAN: Patient will be scheduled to begin a 6-week induction course of intravesical Bacillus Calmette-Guérin (BCG) therapy following this procedure, pending pathology results.
