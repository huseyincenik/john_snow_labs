Title: Operative Report - Cystoscopy and Left Ureteral Stent Exchange
---
PRE-OPERATIVE DIAGNOSIS: Left ureteral stent in place for benign stricture.

POST-OPERATIVE DIAGNOSIS: Same.

PROCEDURE: Cystoscopy and exchange of left ureteral stent.

SURGEON: Dr. Johnson, MD

INDICATION: Patient has an indwelling left ureteral stent for a chronic benign stricture, which requires routine exchange every 4-6 months.

FINDINGS AND PROCEDURE:
Under general anesthesia, the patient was placed in the dorsal lithotomy position. Cystoscopy was performed. The bladder mucosa appeared stable with no new lesions. The existing left ureteral stent was identified.
Using a grasper, the old stent was removed. A guidewire was placed up the left ureter under fluoroscopic guidance. A new 6-French, 24-cm double-J ureteral stent was then advanced over the wire and positioned with its proximal coil in the renal pelvis and its distal coil in the bladder. Position was confirmed with fluoroscopy.

The patient tolerated the procedure well.

PLAN: Continue routine stent exchanges as scheduled.
