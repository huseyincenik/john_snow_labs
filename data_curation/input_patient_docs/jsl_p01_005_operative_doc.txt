Title: CT-Guided Fiducial Marker Placement
---
PRE-PROCEDURE DIAGNOSIS: Prostate Cancer (C61.9)

POST-PROCEDURE DIAGNOSIS: Same

PROCEDURE: CT-guided placement of three periprostatic fiducial markers.

INDICATION: Patient undergoing external beam radiation therapy for prostate cancer. Markers are for image-guided radiation therapy (IGRT).

OPERATOR: Dr. Davis, MD (Interventional Radiology)

PROCEDURE DETAILS:
The patient was placed in the prone position on the CT scanner table. After sterile prep and drape, local anesthesia was administered.
Under CT guidance, three solid gold fiducial markers were placed via a transgluteal approach using 18-gauge needles.
The markers were positioned as follows:
- One marker at the prostatic apex.
- One marker at the right prostatic base.
- One marker at the left prostatic base.

Post-procedure imaging confirmed satisfactory placement of all three markers in the periprostatic tissue.

COMPLICATIONS: None. The patient tolerated the procedure well.

IMPRESSION:
Successful CT-guided placement of three periprostatic fiducial markers in preparation for EBRT.
