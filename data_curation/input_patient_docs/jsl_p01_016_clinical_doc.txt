PATIENT: Patient
ID: p01

SUBJECTIVE:
Patient presents to initiate intravesical therapy for high-grade non-muscle invasive bladder cancer. He has recovered well from his re-staging TURBT on 07/20/2019. Pathology confirmed residual pTa high-grade disease. He has also been formally diagnosed with Lynch Syndrome (MSH2 mutation).

ASSESSMENT:
- High-Grade Non-Muscle Invasive Bladder Cancer (NMIBC), s/p TURBT x2. Ready for adjuvant therapy.
- Lynch Syndrome (MSH2 mutation).

PLAN:
1.  **BCG Therapy:** Begin 6-week induction course of intravesical BCG (Bacillus Calmette-Guérin). Today marks Cycle 1 of 6.
2.  **Procedure:** The patient voided prior to the procedure. 50 mg of BCG in 50 mL saline was instilled into the bladder via a sterile catheter. The catheter was removed. The patient was instructed to retain the solution for 2 hours if possible, changing position every 15 minutes.
3.  **Side Effects:** Counseled on potential side effects, including urinary frequency, urgency, dysuria, hematuria, and low-grade fever. Advised on hygiene measures to prevent transmission.
4.  **Schedule:** He will return weekly for the next 5 weeks to complete the induction course.
5.  **Surveillance:** First surveillance cystoscopy will be scheduled 6 weeks after completion of the induction course.
