EXAMINATION: DXA, BONE DENSITY, AXIAL SKELETON
CLINICAL STATEMENT: 69-year-old male on long-term androgen deprivation therapy for prostate cancer. Baseline evaluation for osteoporosis.

TECHNIQUE: Bone mineral density was measured at the lumbar spine (L1-L4) and dual proximal femurs (femoral neck, total hip).

FINDINGS:
LUMBAR SPINE (L1-L4): The average bone mineral density (BMD) is 0.950 g/cm^2. The T-score is -1.8. The Z-score is -0.5.

LEFT FEMORAL NECK: The BMD is 0.780 g/cm^2. The T-score is -2.1. The Z-score is -0.8.

LEFT TOTAL HIP: The BMD is 0.850 g/cm^2. The T-score is -1.9. The Z-score is -0.7.

RIGHT FEMORAL NECK: The BMD is 0.795 g/cm^2. The T-score is -2.0. The Z-score is -0.7.

RIGHT TOTAL HIP: The BMD is 0.860 g/cm^2. The T-score is -1.8. The Z-score is -0.6.

IMPRESSION:
According to WHO criteria, the findings are consistent with OSTEOPENIA.
- The lowest T-score is -2.1 at the left femoral neck.
- There is no evidence of osteoporosis at this time.

RECOMMENDATION: These results serve as a baseline. Follow-up scanning in 1-2 years is recommended to monitor for changes while on ADT. Clinical management to prevent bone loss should be considered.
