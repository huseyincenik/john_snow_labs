Title: Procedure Note - PSMA PET/CT Guided Prostate Biopsy
---
PRE-PROCEDURE DIAGNOSIS: Local recurrence of prostate cancer.

POST-PROCEDURE DIAGNOSIS: Same.

PROCEDURE: PSMA PET/CT-guided coaxial core needle biopsy of prostate.

INDICATION: To obtain tissue from known site of recurrence for histopathologic confirmation and potential genomic testing, given rising PSA.

OPERATOR: Dr. Marinda, MD (Interventional Radiology)

PROCEDURE DETAILS:
The patient was placed in the prone position. A pre-procedure PSMA PET/CT scan was performed to localize the target.
Under sterile conditions and local anesthesia, and with real-time PET/CT guidance, a coaxial needle was advanced via a transgluteal approach to the margin of the PSMA-avid lesion in the right peripheral zone of the prostate.
Six core biopsy specimens were obtained using an 18-gauge core needle device.
Touch preparations were made and assessed on-site for adequacy, which showed suspicious cells.

COMPLICATIONS: None. The patient tolerated the procedure well.

SPECIMENS SENT:
1. Prostate core biopsy, right peripheral zone (for pathology)
2. Touch preps (for cytology)

IMPRESSION: Successful PSMA PET/CT-guided core biopsy of the recurrent prostate tumor.
