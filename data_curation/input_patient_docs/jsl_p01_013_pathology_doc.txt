VARIANT DETECTED:
Gene: MSH2 (NM_000251)
Variant: c.2038C>T (p.Arg680*)
Zygosity: Heterozygous
Classification: Pathogenic

INTERPRETATION:
This result confirms a diagnosis of Lynch Syndrome. The identified MSH2 nonsense variant (p.Arg680*) is a known pathogenic mutation that leads to a truncated, non-functional protein. Pathogenic variants in MSH2 are associated with a significantly increased lifetime risk for colorectal, endometrial, gastric, ovarian, urothelial, and other cancers.

This finding explains the patient's extensive personal history of multiple primary malignancies.

RECOMMENDATIONS:
-   Lifelong, enhanced surveillance for Lynch-associated cancers is critical.
-   Cascade testing for first-degree relatives is strongly recommended.
-   This information should guide all future oncologic management.
