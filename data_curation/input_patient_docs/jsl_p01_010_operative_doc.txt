Title: Operative Report - Cystoscopy and TURBT

PRE-OPERATIVE DIAGNOSIS: Bladder tumor

POST-OPERATIVE DIAGNOSIS: Bladder tumor

PROCEDURE:
1.  Cystoscopy
2.  Transurethral Resection of Bladder Tumor (TURBT)

SURGEON: Dr. Johnson, MD

ANESTHESIA: General

FINDINGS:
Flexible cystoscopy was performed. The urethra showed some mild stricturing but was traversable. The bladder mucosa showed diffuse post-radiation changes. A solitary, approximately 1.5 cm papillary tumor was identified on the posterior wall, to the left of the midline. The remainder of the bladder was clear.

PROCEDURE DESCRIPTION:
After induction of general anesthesia, the patient was placed in the dorsal lithotomy position. The resectoscope was introduced into the bladder. The papillary tumor identified on the posterior wall was resected in a fractionated manner. Papillary components were resected first, followed by a deep resection of the tumor base to include muscularis propria. Hemostasis was achieved with fulguration. The resected tissue was sent to pathology in separate containers ("papillary tumor" and "deep base"). At the end of the case, the bladder was irrigated clear. A Foley catheter was placed for continuous drainage.

The patient tolerated the procedure well and was transferred to the recovery room in stable condition.

SPECIMENS SENT:
1.  Bladder tumor, papillary component
2.  Bladder tumor, deep base
