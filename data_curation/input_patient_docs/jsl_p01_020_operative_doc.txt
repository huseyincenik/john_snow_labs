Title: Operative Report - Cystoscopy, TUR of Scar, and L Ureteral Stent
---
PRE-OPERATIVE DIAGNOSIS:
1. Left hydronephrosis
2. Bladder outlet obstruction
3. History of bladder cancer

POST-OPERATIVE DIAGNOSIS: Same.

PROCEDURE:
1. Cystoscopy
2. Transurethral resection of scar tissue at left ureteral orifice
3. Left ureteral stent placement

SURGEON: Dr. Johnson, MD

INDICATION: Patient was found to have new moderate left hydronephrosis on a surveillance renal ultrasound. This is presumed to be due to scarring at the left ureteral orifice from his prior TURBT and/or radiation.

FINDINGS AND PROCEDURE:
Cystoscopy revealed significant scar tissue and inflammation at the site of the prior TURBT on the posterior wall, encompassing the left ureteral orifice (UO). The UO was stenotic and could not be cannulated. A decision was made to resect the scar tissue.
Using a resectoscope, the dense scar tissue around the left UO was carefully resected until the orifice was patent. A guidewire was then advanced up the left ureter under fluoroscopic guidance, and a 6-French, 24-cm double-J ureteral stent was placed without difficulty. Good efflux of urine was seen from the stent. The resected scar tissue was sent to pathology.

SPECIMENS SENT:
1. Left trigone scar tissue

PLAN: The stent will remain in place for several weeks to allow the ureter to heal and ensure patency. The patient will return for follow-up and eventual stent removal.
