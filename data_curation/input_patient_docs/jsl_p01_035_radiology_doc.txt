EXAMINATION: NM Ga-68 PSMA PET/MR
CLINICAL STATEMENT: Recurrent prostate cancer, s/p initiation of ADT (Relugolix). PSA has responded from 4.33 to 0.15 ng/mL. Restaging.

TECHNIQUE: Combined PET/MR imaging of the abdomen and pelvis was performed after intravenous injection of 6.71 mCi of Gallium-68 PSMA-11.

COMPARISON: PSMA PET/CT from December 11, 2021.

FINDINGS:
PROSTATE: The previously noted focus of intense PSMA uptake in the right peripheral zone is now markedly decreased in avidity (SUVmax 2.1, previously 8.5). This demonstrates a significant response to androgen deprivation therapy.

LYMPH NODES: No PSMA-avid lymph nodes.

BONES: No PSMA-avid osseous lesions.

OTHER: Left nephrostomy tube is in place. The left kidney shows improved drainage with resolution of the severe hydronephrosis, though some mild residual collecting system dilation persists.

IMPRESSION:
1.  Excellent response of the local prostate cancer recurrence to ADT, with a significant interval decrease in PSMA avidity.
2.  No evidence of regional or distant metastatic disease.
3.  Decompressed left collecting system, status post nephrostomy tube placement.
