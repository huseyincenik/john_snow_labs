This consultation is to establish a comprehensive survivorship care plan, given the patient's multiple cancer diagnoses and complex medical history.

DIAGNOSES OVERVIEW:
1.  **Lynch Syndrome (MSH2 mutation):** The unifying diagnosis. This predisposes him to multiple cancers and requires lifelong, multi-system surveillance.
2.  **Rectal Cancer (1987):** Cured. Long-term sequelae include colostomy and effects of pelvic radiation.
3.  **Appendiceal Cancer (2005):** Cured. No active issues.
4.  **Bladder Cancer (2019):** High-grade NMIBC, s/p TURBT/BCG. Currently NED, but at high risk of recurrence.
5.  **Prostate Cancer (2015):** Recurrent disease, currently well-controlled on ADT (Relugolix).
6.  **Complications:** Chronic left ureteral stricture, currently managed with an internal stent.

SURVEILLANCE PLAN:
-   **Urology (Dr. Johnson):**
    -   Bladder Cancer: Surveillance cystoscopy and urine cytology every 6 months for 2 years, then annually.
    -   Prostate Cancer: PSA and testosterone levels every 6 months while on ADT.
    -   Upper Tracts: Annual imaging (CT Urogram or MR Urogram) to monitor for urothelial carcinoma of the kidneys/ureters, a known risk with Lynch Syndrome.
-   **Gastroenterology:**
    -   High-definition colonoscopy (via stoma) every 1-2 years.
-   **Medical Oncology (Dr. Smith):**
    -   Manage ADT for prostate cancer. Monitor for side effects (bone health, metabolic changes).
-   **Dermatology:**
    -   Annual full-body skin exam for Lynch-associated skin tumors (sebaceous neoplasms).

COORDINATION OF CARE:
-   Patient will continue to see his specialists as outlined above.
-   This summary will be shared with all members of his care team.
-   Patient is encouraged to remain active and has been referred to our cancer rehabilitation service to address long-term treatment side effects.
